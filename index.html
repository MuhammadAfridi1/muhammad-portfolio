About
Muhammad

I was born in 2005 in Peshawar, Pakistan, and am proud to belong to the Afridi clan from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. My passion for computer science has been a driving force throughout my life.

I completed my high schooling at Government Higher Secondary School, Samabadaber, with an impressive 90% marks. Following that, I enrolled in a bachelor's degree in Computer Science at the University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Peshawar.

In addition to my formal education, I have undertaken various online courses, the details of which can be found in the Certifications section. My language skills include complete command of English, Urdu, and Pashto, and I am at an upper-intermediate level in German. I am also proficient in Microsoft tools such as Word and Excel.

My experiences extend beyond academics. I have volunteered as a tutor, helping students with their studies. Additionally, I participated in football tournaments, though I chose to step away from the sport due to personal preferences.
